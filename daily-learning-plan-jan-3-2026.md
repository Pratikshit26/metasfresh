## Time Management Tips

⏰ **Pomodoro Technique**: 25-min focus + 5-min break  
🎯 **Single Task Focus**: Complete one feature before moving to next  
📝 **Document as You Go**: Don't wait until end of day  
🔄 **Git Commits**: Commit after each logical unit of work  
💬 **Seek Help**: Don't spend more than 30 min stuck on one issue  
🌟 **Celebrate Wins**: Acknowledge each milestone achieved
